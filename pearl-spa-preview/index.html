✦ About Pearl Spa & Sauna

San Francisco's Premier Women's-Only Spa

Opened in 2016, Pearl Spa combines Korean beauty traditions with American relaxation. A unique two-story women's-only spa and Korean-style jimjilbang bathhouse located in San Francisco.

Our dedicated team of expert therapists is here to help you achieve peace of mind in a serene atmosphere. From invigorating body scrubs and seaweed gel body wraps to relaxing massages tailored to your needs, every treatment includes 2 hours amenities access.
