About Us

Where Art Meets Dentistry

At Laurel Village Dental Loft, we believe dental care should be an exquisite experience. Our boutique practice combines the latest technology — handheld digital X-rays, 3D intraoral scanning, and state-of-the-art equipment — with a warm, compassionate approach that puts you at ease.

Led by Dr. Bordow, our team is dedicated to providing meticulous, personalized care. From the moment you walk through our doors, you'll feel the difference that genuine empathy and attention to detail can make.
